Public domain status of this voice pack's sources

The Piper voice used for these clips (en_US-kristin-medium) was trained by Bryce Beattie on
recordings from LibriVox (https://librivox.org). LibriVox recordings are in the public domain in the
United States: https://librivox.org/pages/public-domain/

The model author states the voice's license as "public domain" and imposes no further license or
restriction: https://brycebeattie.com/files/tts/
Model card: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_US/kristin/medium/MODEL_CARD

No license text is owed for a public-domain source, so this file records the status in place of one.
The clips in this folder are that voice's synthesized output.
